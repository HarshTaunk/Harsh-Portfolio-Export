A bit more about me

I’m a Computer Science graduate from IIIT Vadodara ICD. Over the past few years, I’ve worked on consumer apps, IoT products, legal tech, healthcare and enterprise tools.

I’m curious about how people make decisions, how technology can actually help (especially with AI), and how good design can make complex things feel simple.

When I’m not designing, you’ll probably find me exploring new tools, planning my next trip, or obsessing over pizza.
